# Tomatoes on the balcony

Plant seedlings deep, burying the stem up to the first leaves: tomatoes grow roots along the buried stem. Use pots of at least 20 litres and stake or cage the plants early.

Water deeply and regularly at the base, never on the leaves. Irregular watering causes blossom end rot (black, leathery patches at the bottom of the fruit) and split skins.

Early blight shows as brown spots with yellow rings on the lower leaves. Remove affected leaves, mulch the soil so spores do not splash up, and leave space between plants for air to flow. Pinch out side shoots of cordon varieties so the plant puts its energy into fruit.
