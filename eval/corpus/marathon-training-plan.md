# Marathon plan, 16 weeks

Run four or five days a week. The weekly long run grows from 14 km to 32 km by week 13, always at an easy conversational pace. Tuesday is tempo: 20-40 minutes at half-marathon effort. Thursday is intervals, for example 6 × 800 m with 400 m jogs.

Increase weekly distance by no more than about 10% and make every fourth week a lighter recovery week.

Taper over the last three weeks: cut volume by 20%, 40% and 60% but keep some race-pace running. Practise race-day fuelling on long runs: a gel every 40 minutes with water. Do not try new shoes on race day.
