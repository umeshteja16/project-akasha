# Sleeping better

Keep the same wake-up time every day, including weekends: it anchors the circadian rhythm more than the bedtime does. Get daylight in the morning, ideally outside.

Caffeine has a half-life of about five to six hours, so stop coffee and tea after early afternoon. Alcohol helps you fall asleep but makes sleep lighter in the second half of the night.

Keep the bedroom cool, dark and quiet. Dim screens for an hour before bed. If you lie awake for more than about 20 minutes, get up and read in low light until sleepy rather than lying there worrying.
