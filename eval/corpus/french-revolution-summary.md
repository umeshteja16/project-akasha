# The French Revolution in brief

By 1789 France was nearly bankrupt after costly wars, and bread prices were soaring. Louis XVI called the Estates-General for the first time since 1614. The Third Estate broke away, declared itself the National Assembly and swore the Tennis Court Oath not to disband until France had a constitution.

On 14 July 1789 crowds stormed the Bastille. In August the Assembly abolished feudal privileges and adopted the Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen.

The monarchy was abolished in 1792 and the king executed in January 1793. The Terror under Robespierre followed, ending with his own execution in 1794. Napoleon seized power in 1799.
