Background processing and job resilience guidelines. When worker nodes crash due to hardware failures or unexpected memory exhaustion, they terminate the current event loop. To recover from these crashed background jobs, supervisor processes auto-restart the node. Jobs can then be recovered safely from the active queue because their state is persisted in Redis. Make every job idempotent: a job that ran halfway before the crash will run again from the start, so writes must be safe to repeat. Poison messages that crash a worker every time should be moved to a dead-letter queue after a fixed number of attempts instead of retrying forever.
