# Espresso machine maintenance

Daily: purge the steam wand after every use and wipe it, and rinse the portafilter and basket. Flush the group head with water after the last shot.

Weekly: backflush with a blind basket and plain water; monthly with espresso machine cleaning powder. Soak the baskets and the shower screen.

Descale every two to three months, more often with hard water, using a descaler made for espresso machines (not vinegar, which can damage seals). Better still, use filtered or softened water so scale does not build up in the boiler. Replace the group head gasket once a year or when shots start leaking around the portafilter.
