# Home composting

Mix "greens" (vegetable scraps, coffee grounds, fresh grass clippings) with "browns" (dry leaves, cardboard, straw) at roughly one part green to two or three parts brown by volume. That keeps the carbon to nitrogen ratio near 30:1, which the microbes like.

Turn the pile every week or two to add oxygen. A smelly, slimy heap is too wet and has too many greens: add shredded cardboard. A heap that does nothing is too dry: water it until it feels like a wrung-out sponge.

Do not add meat, dairy or oily food, which attract rats. Red wiggler worms in a closed bin turn kitchen scraps into fine compost in a small flat.
