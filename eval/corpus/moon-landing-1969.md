# Apollo 11

Apollo 11 launched on 16 July 1969 on a Saturn V rocket with Neil Armstrong, Buzz Aldrin and Michael Collins. On 20 July the lunar module Eagle landed in the Sea of Tranquility while Collins stayed in orbit aboard the command module Columbia.

During the descent, computer alarms (1202 and 1201) warned that the guidance computer was overloaded, and Armstrong took manual control to fly past a boulder field, landing with less than a minute of fuel to spare.

Armstrong stepped onto the surface about six and a half hours later. The crew collected 21.5 kg of rock and dust, set up experiments including a laser retroreflector, and returned safely to Earth on 24 July.
