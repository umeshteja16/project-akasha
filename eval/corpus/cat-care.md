# Looking after the new kitten

Food: kitten food four times a day until three months old, then three times until six months. Fresh water always; cats often prefer a fountain. No cow's milk, it upsets their stomach.

Vet: first vaccinations at 8-9 weeks and a booster at 12 weeks, plus worming and flea treatment. Book neutering at around five months. A microchip is required for the pet passport.

Litter: one tray per cat plus one, in quiet places, scooped daily. Scratching posts near the sofa save the furniture. Play with a wand toy twice a day; a bored kitten bites ankles.
