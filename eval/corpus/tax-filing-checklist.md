# Yearly tax return checklist

Collect before starting:

- income statements from every employer and bank interest statements
- receipts for deductible expenses: work equipment, home office, professional courses, donations
- health insurance and pension contribution statements
- last year's return, for reference

The filing deadline is the end of July unless an adviser files for you. Keep receipts for at least ten years in case of an audit. Scan paper receipts into the "taxes" folder right away so nothing gets lost.
