Physiotherapy notes: runner's knee (patellofemoral pain)

Pain around and behind the kneecap that gets worse on stairs, squats and downhill running. Usually from too much load too soon, plus weak hips and quads.

Plan from the physio: reduce running volume for two weeks but keep moving (cycling and swimming are fine). Ice for 15 minutes after activity if it is sore. Strengthening three times a week: glute bridges, side-lying leg raises, wall sits, step-downs from a low box, slowly. Return to running with run-walk intervals on flat ground and increase only if pain stays below 3 out of 10 the next morning.
