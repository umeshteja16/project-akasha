# Why is my bread dense?

A dense, gummy crumb usually means under-proofing: the dough did not ferment long enough before baking. Do the poke test: a gentle finger dent should spring back slowly and leave a small dimple.

Other causes:

- Weak gluten development. Add a few sets of stretch and folds during bulk fermentation.
- An inactive leaven. Only bake when the starter has doubled since its last feeding.
- Cutting the loaf while hot. The crumb keeps setting for an hour as it cools.

Poor oven spring often comes from a cold oven. Preheat a Dutch oven for 45 minutes at 250 °C and bake covered for the first 20 minutes so steam keeps the crust soft while the loaf rises.
