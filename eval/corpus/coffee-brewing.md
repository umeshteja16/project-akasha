# Pour-over coffee

Ratio: 1 g of coffee to 16 g of water (for one mug, 15 g coffee and 250 g water). Grind medium-fine, like coarse sand. Water just off the boil, around 93-96 °C.

Bloom first: pour twice the coffee's weight in water, wait 30-45 seconds while the gas escapes, then pour the rest in slow circles. Total brew time about three minutes.

Sour and thin means under-extracted: grind finer or use hotter water. Bitter and harsh means over-extracted: grind coarser. Fresh beans, roasted within the last month, matter more than any gadget.
