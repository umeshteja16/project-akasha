Bird log, river walk

April 12: grey heron fishing at the weir, two great crested grebes displaying, first chiffchaff of the year calling from the willows.
April 26: blackcap and willow warbler singing; a kingfisher flew low upstream, a flash of blue.
May 3: swifts are back over the old town, screaming in groups. Common sandpiper on the gravel bar.
May 17: cuckoo heard from the far meadow. A pair of mute swans with six cygnets.

Notes: the 8x42 binoculars are much better in low light than the old compact pair. Go before 7 am, when the path is quiet.
