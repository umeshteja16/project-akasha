# Japan trip, spring

**Days 1-4, Tokyo:** Asakusa and Senso-ji early in the morning, teamLab, a day trip to Nikko for the shrines.

**Days 5-8, Kyoto:** Fushimi Inari at sunrise before the crowds, the bamboo grove in Arashiyama, a night in a ryokan with a kaiseki dinner and an onsen. Day trip to Nara to see the deer and Todai-ji.

**Day 9, Osaka:** street food in Dotonbori, then fly home from Kansai.

The rail pass is only worth it with the Tokyo to Kyoto bullet train plus the Nikko trip; buy it before leaving. Carry cash: many small restaurants and temples do not take cards.
