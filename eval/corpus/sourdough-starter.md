# Keeping a sourdough starter alive

Feed the starter twice a day at room temperature: discard all but 50 g, then add 50 g of flour and 50 g of water (100% hydration). A healthy starter doubles in four to six hours and smells pleasantly sour, like yoghurt.

If it smells of nail polish remover it is hungry; feed it more often or use a higher ratio of fresh flour. A grey liquid on top ("hooch") is harmless: stir it in or pour it off.

For a break, keep the starter in the fridge and feed it once a week. Give it two room-temperature feedings before baking so the levain is active again. Rye flour wakes a sluggish starter faster than white flour.
