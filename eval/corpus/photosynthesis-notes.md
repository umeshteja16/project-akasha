# Photosynthesis (biology revision)

Plants turn light energy into chemical energy: carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen.

**Light-dependent reactions** happen in the thylakoid membranes of the chloroplast. Chlorophyll absorbs mostly red and blue light; water is split, releasing oxygen, and the energy is stored as ATP and NADPH.

**Light-independent reactions (Calvin cycle)** happen in the stroma. The enzyme RuBisCO fixes carbon dioxide, and ATP and NADPH from the first stage are used to build sugars.

The rate is limited by light intensity, carbon dioxide concentration and temperature, whichever is in shortest supply.
