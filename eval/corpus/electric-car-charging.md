# Charging the electric car

At home the car charges from a 7 kW wallbox overnight: about 40 km of range per hour of charging, so a full charge from 10% takes around eight hours. The off-peak tariff between midnight and 5 am costs a third of the day rate, so the charge is scheduled to start at midnight.

For daily driving keep the battery between 20% and 80%; charge to 100% only right before long trips.

On motorway trips use DC fast chargers. Charging speed drops sharply above 80%, so several short stops are quicker than one long one. Precondition the battery by navigating to the charger in winter, otherwise a cold battery charges slowly.
